# Street address assignment

Document ID: service-addr-assign  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `addr-assign`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Urban Planning and Permits (DUPP)  
**Fee:** 3,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 7 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- cadastral extract

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for street address assignment are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for street address assignment is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the street address assignment application has begun.
