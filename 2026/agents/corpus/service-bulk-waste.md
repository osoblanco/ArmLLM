# Bulk waste collection booking

Document ID: service-bulk-waste  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `bulk-waste`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Waste Management (DWM)  
**Fee:** 5,500 AMD  
**Processing time:** 5 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- address
- item list

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for bulk waste collection booking are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for bulk waste collection booking is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the bulk waste collection booking application has begun.
