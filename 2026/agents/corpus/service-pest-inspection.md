# Commercial pest inspection

Document ID: service-pest-inspection  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `pest-inspection`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Public Health Inspection (DPHI)  
**Fee:** 9,500 AMD  
**Processing time:** 7 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- premises address
- business licence number

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for commercial pest inspection are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for commercial pest inspection is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the commercial pest inspection application has begun.
