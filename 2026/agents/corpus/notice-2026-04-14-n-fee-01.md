# Revised fee schedule — Residential parking permit

Document ID: n-fee-01  
Published: 2026-04-14  
Source: Dvin Municipality

By decision of the Dvin Municipal Council, the fee for the service listed under reference `parking-permit` is revised from 8,000 AMD to 11,000 AMD, effective 2026-04-14. Applications submitted on or after that date are charged at the revised rate. Earlier published schedules are superseded to the extent of this revision.
