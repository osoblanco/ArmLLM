# Leak inspection request

Document ID: service-leak-report  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `leak-report`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Water and Utilities (DWU)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 2 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- address

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for leak inspection request are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for leak inspection request is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the leak inspection request application has begun.
