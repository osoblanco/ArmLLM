# Department of Civil Registry

Document ID: dept-dcr  
Published: 2026-01-08  
Source: Dvin Municipality

The Department of Civil Registry (DCR) is responsible for the services listed below.

**Head of department:** Anahit Sargsyan  
**Location:** Central Service Hall, floor 2  
**Email:** dcr@dvin.gov.am  
**Opening hours:** Monday to Friday 08:30-16:30; Saturday 10:00-14:00

## Services administered by DCR

| Service | Reference | Fee | Processing time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate issuance | `birth-cert` | 2,000 AMD | 5 working days |
| Marriage registration | `marriage-reg` | 5,000 AMD | 10 working days |
| Legal name change | `name-change` | 12,000 AMD | 30 working days |
| Certificate of residence | `residence-cert` | 1,500 AMD | 3 working days |
| Death certificate issuance | `death-cert` | 2,000 AMD | 3 working days |
