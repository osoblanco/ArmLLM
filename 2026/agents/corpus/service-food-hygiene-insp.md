# Food hygiene inspection

Document ID: service-food-hygiene-insp  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `food-hygiene-insp`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Public Health Inspection (DPHI)  
**Fee:** 11,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 7 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- food service licence

## Prerequisite

Before applying for food hygiene inspection, applicants must already hold a valid **Food service licence** (reference `food-license`), administered by Department of Business Licensing.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for food hygiene inspection are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for food hygiene inspection is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the food hygiene inspection application has begun.
