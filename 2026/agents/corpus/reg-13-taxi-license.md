# Municipal Regulation 13 — Taxi operator licence

Document ID: reg-13  
Published: 2025-11-12  
Source: Dvin Municipality

## Article 13.1 — Scope

This article governs the administration of taxi operator licence within Dvin.

## Article 13.2 — Time limits

The responsible department shall decide within 21 working days of receiving a complete application. Where additional information is requested, the period is suspended until that information is supplied.

## Article 13.3 — Appeals

An applicant may appeal a refusal within 30 calendar days of notification. Appeals are heard by the municipal review board.
