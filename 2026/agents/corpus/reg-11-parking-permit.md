# Municipal Regulation 11 — Residential parking permit

Document ID: reg-11  
Published: 2025-11-12  
Source: Dvin Municipality

## Article 11.1 — Scope

This article governs the administration of residential parking permit within Dvin.

## Article 11.2 — Time limits

The responsible department shall decide within 7 working days of receiving a complete application. Where additional information is requested, the period is suspended until that information is supplied.

## Article 11.3 — Appeals

An applicant may appeal a refusal within 30 calendar days of notification. Appeals are heard by the municipal review board.
