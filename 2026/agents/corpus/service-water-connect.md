# New water connection

Document ID: service-water-connect  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `water-connect`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Water and Utilities (DWU)  
**Fee:** 55,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- land title
- site plan
- plumbing schematic

## Prerequisite

Before applying for new water connection, applicants must already hold a valid **Zoning certificate** (reference `zoning-cert`), administered by Department of Urban Planning and Permits.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for new water connection are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for new water connection is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the new water connection application has begun.
