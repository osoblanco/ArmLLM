# Facade alteration permit

Document ID: service-facade-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `facade-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Urban Planning and Permits (DUPP)  
**Fee:** 25,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- photographs of existing facade
- proposed elevation drawing

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for facade alteration permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for facade alteration permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the facade alteration permit application has begun.
