# Skip placement permit

Document ID: service-skip-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `skip-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Waste Management (DWM)  
**Fee:** 14,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 7 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- placement plan
- duration statement

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for skip placement permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for skip placement permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the skip placement permit application has begun.
