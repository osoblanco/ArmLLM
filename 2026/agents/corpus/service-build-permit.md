# Construction permit

Document ID: service-build-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `build-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Urban Planning and Permits (DUPP)  
**Fee:** 85,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 45 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- site plan
- architectural drawings
- land title
- geotechnical report

## Prerequisite

Before applying for construction permit, applicants must already hold a valid **Zoning certificate** (reference `zoning-cert`), administered by Department of Urban Planning and Permits.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for construction permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for construction permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the construction permit application has begun.
