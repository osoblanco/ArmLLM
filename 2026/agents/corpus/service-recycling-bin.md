# Commercial recycling bin allocation

Document ID: service-recycling-bin  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `recycling-bin`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Waste Management (DWM)  
**Fee:** 7,500 AMD  
**Processing time:** 10 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- business licence number

## Prerequisite

Before applying for commercial recycling bin allocation, applicants must already hold a valid **General business licence** (reference `business-license`), administered by Department of Business Licensing.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for commercial recycling bin allocation are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for commercial recycling bin allocation is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the commercial recycling bin allocation application has begun.
