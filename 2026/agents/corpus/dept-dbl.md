# Department of Business Licensing

Document ID: dept-dbl  
Published: 2026-01-08  
Source: Dvin Municipality

The Department of Business Licensing (DBL) is responsible for the services listed below.

**Head of department:** Nune Harutyunyan  
**Location:** Central Service Hall, floor 4  
**Email:** dbl@dvin.gov.am  
**Opening hours:** Monday to Friday 08:30-16:30; Saturday closed

## Services administered by DBL

| Service | Reference | Fee | Processing time |
|---|---|---|---|
| General business licence | `business-license` | 60,000 AMD | 21 working days |
| Food service licence | `food-license` | 75,000 AMD | 30 working days |
| Outdoor seating permit | `outdoor-seating` | 30,000 AMD | 14 working days |
| Commercial signage permit | `signage-permit` | 20,000 AMD | 14 working days |
| Municipal market stall licence | `market-stall` | 16,000 AMD | 10 working days |
