# Taxi operator licence

Document ID: service-taxi-license  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `taxi-license`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Transport and Roads (DTR)  
**Fee:** 40,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- driving licence
- vehicle inspection certificate
- criminal record extract

## Prerequisite

Before applying for taxi operator licence, applicants must already hold a valid **Municipal vehicle inspection** (reference `vehicle-inspection`), administered by Department of Transport and Roads.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for taxi operator licence are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for taxi operator licence is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the taxi operator licence application has begun.
