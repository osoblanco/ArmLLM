# Municipal disability travel pass

Document ID: service-disability-pass  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `disability-pass`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Social Support (DSS)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- medical assessment
- identity document

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for municipal disability travel pass are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for municipal disability travel pass is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the municipal disability travel pass application has begun.
