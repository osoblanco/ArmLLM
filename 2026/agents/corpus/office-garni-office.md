- Bulk waste collection booking (`bulk-waste`)
- General business licence (`business-license`)
- Commercial signage permit (`signage-permit`)

- School facility hire (`facility-hire`)
- Street performance licence (`street-performance`)
- Food hygiene inspection (`food-hygiene-insp`)

## Access

Step-free access is available at the main entrance. Appointments are recommended for services requiring document verification.
