# Marriage registration

Document ID: service-marriage-reg  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `marriage-reg`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Civil Registry (DCR)  
**Fee:** 5,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 10 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- identity documents of both parties
- residence confirmation

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for marriage registration are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for marriage registration is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the marriage registration application has begun.
