# Birth certificate issuance

Document ID: service-birth-cert  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `birth-cert`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Civil Registry (DCR)  
**Fee:** 2,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 5 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- hospital record
- parent identity document

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for birth certificate issuance are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for birth certificate issuance is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the birth certificate issuance application has begun.
