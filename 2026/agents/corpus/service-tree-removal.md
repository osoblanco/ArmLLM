# Tree removal authorisation

Document ID: service-tree-removal  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `tree-removal`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Parks and Green Space (DPGS)  
**Fee:** 18,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- arborist report
- site photographs

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for tree removal authorisation are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for tree removal authorisation is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the tree removal authorisation application has begun.
