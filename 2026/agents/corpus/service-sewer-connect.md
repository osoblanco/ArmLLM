# Sewer connection approval

Document ID: service-sewer-connect  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `sewer-connect`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Water and Utilities (DWU)  
**Fee:** 48,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- site plan
- drainage calculation

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for sewer connection approval are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for sewer connection approval is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the sewer connection approval application has begun.
