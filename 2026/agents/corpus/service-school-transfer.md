# School district transfer request

Document ID: service-school-transfer  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `school-transfer`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Education Facilities (DEF)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- proof of residence
- current school record

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for school district transfer request are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for school district transfer request is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the school district transfer request application has begun.
