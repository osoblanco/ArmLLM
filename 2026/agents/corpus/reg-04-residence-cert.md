# Municipal Regulation 4 — Certificate of residence

Document ID: reg-04  
Published: 2025-11-12  
Source: Dvin Municipality

## Article 4.1 — Scope

This article governs the administration of certificate of residence within Dvin.

## Article 4.2 — Time limits

The responsible department shall decide within 3 working days of receiving a complete application. Where additional information is requested, the period is suspended until that information is supplied.

## Article 4.3 — Appeals

An applicant may appeal a refusal within 30 calendar days of notification. Appeals are heard by the municipal review board.
