# Municipal market stall licence

Document ID: service-market-stall  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `market-stall`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Business Licensing (DBL)  
**Fee:** 16,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 10 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- identity document
- product list

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for municipal market stall licence are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for municipal market stall licence is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the municipal market stall licence application has begun.
