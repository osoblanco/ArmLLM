# Bus stop relocation request

Document ID: service-bus-stop-request  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `bus-stop-request`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Transport and Roads (DTR)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 60 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- petition with 25 signatures

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for bus stop relocation request are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for bus stop relocation request is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the bus stop relocation request application has begun.
