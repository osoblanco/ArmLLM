# Street performance licence

Document ID: service-street-performance  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `street-performance`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Cultural Affairs (DCA)  
**Fee:** 6,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 7 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- identity document
- performance description

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for street performance licence are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for street performance licence is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the street performance licence application has begun.
