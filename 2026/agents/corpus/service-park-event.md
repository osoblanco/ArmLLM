# Park event permit

Document ID: service-park-event  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `park-event`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Parks and Green Space (DPGS)  
**Fee:** 22,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- event plan
- public liability insurance

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for park event permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for park event permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the park event permit application has begun.
