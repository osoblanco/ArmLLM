# Change of opening hours — Yervandashat Citizen Office

Document ID: n-hours-02  
Published: 2026-06-18  
Source: Dvin Municipality

With effect from 2026-06-18, weekday opening hours at the premises identified by reference `yervandashat-office` change from 09:00-17:00 to 10:00-15:00. Saturday arrangements are unaffected. Notices displayed at the entrance take precedence over printed leaflets issued before this date.
