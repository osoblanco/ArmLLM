# Fire safety certificate

Document ID: service-fire-safety-cert  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `fire-safety-cert`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Emergency Preparedness (DEP)  
**Fee:** 28,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- floor plan
- evacuation plan
- equipment inventory

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for fire safety certificate are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for fire safety certificate is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the fire safety certificate application has begun.
