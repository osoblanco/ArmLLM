# Zoning certificate

Document ID: service-zoning-cert  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `zoning-cert`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Urban Planning and Permits (DUPP)  
**Fee:** 15,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- land title
- cadastral extract

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for zoning certificate are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for zoning certificate is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the zoning certificate application has begun.
