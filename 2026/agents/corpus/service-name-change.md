# Legal name change

Document ID: service-name-change  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `name-change`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Civil Registry (DCR)  
**Fee:** 12,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- identity document
- notarised application
- court decision

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for legal name change are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for legal name change is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the legal name change application has begun.
