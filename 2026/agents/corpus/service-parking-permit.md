# Residential parking permit

Document ID: service-parking-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `parking-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Transport and Roads (DTR)  
**Fee:** 8,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 7 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- vehicle registration
- proof of residence

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for residential parking permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for residential parking permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the residential parking permit application has begun.
