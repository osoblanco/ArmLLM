# Public assembly notification

Document ID: service-assembly-notify  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `assembly-notify`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Emergency Preparedness (DEP)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 5 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- route plan
- organiser contact details

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for public assembly notification are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for public assembly notification is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the public assembly notification application has begun.
