# Certificate of residence

Document ID: service-residence-cert  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `residence-cert`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Civil Registry (DCR)  
**Fee:** 1,500 AMD  
**Processing time:** 3 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- identity document
- utility bill

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for certificate of residence are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for certificate of residence is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the certificate of residence application has begun.
