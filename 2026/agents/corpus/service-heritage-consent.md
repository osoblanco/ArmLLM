# Heritage building works consent

Document ID: service-heritage-consent  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `heritage-consent`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Cultural Affairs (DCA)  
**Fee:** Fee is set annually by Council decision and is not published in this schedule.  
**Processing time:** 45 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- conservation statement
- measured survey

## Prerequisite

Before applying for heritage building works consent, applicants must already hold a valid **Zoning certificate** (reference `zoning-cert`), administered by Department of Urban Planning and Permits.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for heritage building works consent are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for heritage building works consent is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the heritage building works consent application has begun.
