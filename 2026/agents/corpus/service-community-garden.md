# Community garden allocation

Document ID: service-community-garden  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `community-garden`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Parks and Green Space (DPGS)  
**Fee:** 4,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- applicant list
- cultivation plan

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for community garden allocation are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for community garden allocation is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the community garden allocation application has begun.
