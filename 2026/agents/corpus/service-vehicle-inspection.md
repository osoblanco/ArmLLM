# Municipal vehicle inspection

Document ID: service-vehicle-inspection  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `vehicle-inspection`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Transport and Roads (DTR)  
**Fee:** 6,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 2 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- vehicle registration

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for municipal vehicle inspection are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for municipal vehicle inspection is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the municipal vehicle inspection application has begun.
