# Heritage note — Anania Shirakatsi and the Questions and Solutions

Document ID: heritage-anania-shirakatsi  
Published: 2026-03-11  
Source: Dvin Municipality

Anania Shirakatsi, the seventh-century Armenian mathematician, astronomer and geographer, compiled the *Questions and Solutions* (Խնդիրք եւ լուծմունք), the oldest surviving Armenian collection of mathematical problems. Its distinctive feature is that the problems are published together with their solutions.

Several of the problems are drawn from ordinary administrative life of the period: shares of an inheritance, quantities of grain, distances between towns, the division of a payment. Shirakatsi also compiled the geography known as the *Ashkharhatsuyts*.

The municipal archive holds a facsimile edition, available for consultation at the Central Service Hall by appointment.
