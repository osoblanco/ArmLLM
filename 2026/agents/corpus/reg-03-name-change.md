# Municipal Regulation 3 — Legal name change

Document ID: reg-03  
Published: 2025-11-12  
Source: Dvin Municipality

## Article 3.1 — Scope

This article governs the administration of legal name change within Dvin.

## Article 3.2 — Time limits

The responsible department shall decide within 30 working days of receiving a complete application. Where additional information is requested, the period is suspended until that information is supplied.

## Article 3.3 — Appeals

An applicant may appeal a refusal within 30 calendar days of notification. Appeals are heard by the municipal review board.
