# Temporary road closure authorisation

Document ID: service-road-closure  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `road-closure`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Transport and Roads (DTR)  
**Fee:** Fee is set annually by Council decision and is not published in this schedule.  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- traffic management plan
- insurance certificate

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for temporary road closure authorisation are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for temporary road closure authorisation is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the temporary road closure authorisation application has begun.
