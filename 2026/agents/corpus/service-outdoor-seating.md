# Outdoor seating permit

Document ID: service-outdoor-seating  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `outdoor-seating`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Business Licensing (DBL)  
**Fee:** 30,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- business licence
- pavement layout drawing

## Prerequisite

Before applying for outdoor seating permit, applicants must already hold a valid **General business licence** (reference `business-license`), administered by Department of Business Licensing.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for outdoor seating permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for outdoor seating permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the outdoor seating permit application has begun.
