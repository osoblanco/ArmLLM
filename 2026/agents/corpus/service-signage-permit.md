# Commercial signage permit

Document ID: service-signage-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `signage-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Business Licensing (DBL)  
**Fee:** 20,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- signage drawing
- building owner consent

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for commercial signage permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for commercial signage permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the commercial signage permit application has begun.
