# Public filming permit

Document ID: service-filming-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `filming-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Cultural Affairs (DCA)  
**Fee:** 38,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 14 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- shooting schedule
- insurance certificate
- location list

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for public filming permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for public filming permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the public filming permit application has begun.
