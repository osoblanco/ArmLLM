# Change of opening hours — Garni Citizen Office

Document ID: n-hours-01  
Published: 2026-02-11  
Source: Dvin Municipality

With effect from 2026-02-11, weekday opening hours at the premises identified by reference `garni-office` change from 08:00-16:00 to 08:00-18:00. Saturday arrangements are unaffected. Notices displayed at the entrance take precedence over printed leaflets issued before this date.
