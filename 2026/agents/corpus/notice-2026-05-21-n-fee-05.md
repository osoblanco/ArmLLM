# Revised fee schedule — Zoning certificate

Document ID: n-fee-05  
Published: 2026-05-21  
Source: Dvin Municipality

By decision of the Dvin Municipal Council, the fee for the service listed under reference `zoning-cert` is revised from 15,000 AMD to 19,500 AMD, effective 2026-05-21. Applications submitted on or after that date are charged at the revised rate. Earlier published schedules are superseded to the extent of this revision.
