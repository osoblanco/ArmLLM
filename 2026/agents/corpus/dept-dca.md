# Department of Cultural Affairs

Document ID: dept-dca  
Published: 2026-01-08  
Source: Dvin Municipality

The Department of Cultural Affairs (DCA) is responsible for the services listed below.

**Head of department:** Ashot Melkonyan  
**Location:** Central Service Hall, floor 6  
**Email:** dca@dvin.gov.am  
**Opening hours:** Monday to Friday 08:30-16:30; Saturday closed

## Services administered by DCA

| Service | Reference | Fee | Processing time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public filming permit | `filming-permit` | 38,000 AMD | 14 working days |
| Heritage building works consent | `heritage-consent` | Fee is set annually by Council decision and is not published in this schedule. | 45 working days |
| Street performance licence | `street-performance` | 6,000 AMD | 7 working days |
