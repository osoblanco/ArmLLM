# General business licence

Document ID: service-business-license  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `business-license`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Business Licensing (DBL)  
**Fee:** 60,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- registration certificate
- tax identification number
- premises lease

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for general business licence are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for general business licence is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the general business licence application has begun.
