# Death certificate issuance

Document ID: service-death-cert  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `death-cert`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Civil Registry (DCR)  
**Fee:** 2,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 3 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- medical certificate
- applicant identity document

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for death certificate issuance are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for death certificate issuance is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the death certificate issuance application has begun.
