# Water meter replacement

Document ID: service-water-meter  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `water-meter`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Water and Utilities (DWU)  
**Fee:** 9,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 10 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- account number
- identity document

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for water meter replacement are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for water meter replacement is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the water meter replacement application has begun.
