# Public pool water test

Document ID: service-pool-water-test  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `pool-water-test`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Public Health Inspection (DPHI)  
**Fee:** 13,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 5 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- facility registration

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for public pool water test are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for public pool water test is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the public pool water test application has begun.
