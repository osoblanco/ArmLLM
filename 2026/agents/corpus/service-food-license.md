# Food service licence

Document ID: service-food-license  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `food-license`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Business Licensing (DBL)  
**Fee:** 75,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- business licence
- kitchen layout
- staff health certificates

## Prerequisite

Before applying for food service licence, applicants must already hold a valid **General business licence** (reference `business-license`), administered by Department of Business Licensing.

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for food service licence are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for food service licence is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the food service licence application has begun.
