# Childcare subsidy application

Document ID: service-childcare-subsidy  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `childcare-subsidy`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Social Support (DSS)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 21 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- income statement
- childcare enrolment confirmation

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)

## Notes

Applications for childcare subsidy application are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for childcare subsidy application is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the childcare subsidy application application has begun.
