# Housing support allowance

Document ID: service-housing-support  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `housing-support`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Social Support (DSS)  
**Fee:** 0 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** yes

## Required documents

- income statement
- household composition certificate
- tenancy agreement

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Bagaran Citizen Office, 3 Movses Khorenatsi Lane (Bagaran)
- Yervandashat Citizen Office, 22 Sahak Partev Street (Yervandashat)

## Notes

Applications for housing support allowance are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for housing support allowance is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the housing support allowance application has begun.
