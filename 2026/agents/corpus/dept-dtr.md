# Department of Transport and Roads

Document ID: dept-dtr  
Published: 2026-01-08  
Source: Dvin Municipality

The Department of Transport and Roads (DTR) is responsible for the services listed below.

**Head of department:** Gohar Avetisyan  
**Location:** Central Service Hall, floor 3  
**Email:** dtr@dvin.gov.am  
**Opening hours:** Monday to Friday 09:00-17:00; Saturday 10:00-14:00

## Services administered by DTR

| Service | Reference | Fee | Processing time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential parking permit | `parking-permit` | 8,000 AMD | 7 working days |
| Temporary road closure authorisation | `road-closure` | Fee is set annually by Council decision and is not published in this schedule. | 14 working days |
| Taxi operator licence | `taxi-license` | 40,000 AMD | 21 working days |
| Municipal vehicle inspection | `vehicle-inspection` | 6,000 AMD | 2 working days |
| Bus stop relocation request | `bus-stop-request` | 0 AMD | 60 working days |
