# Revised fee schedule — Municipal market stall licence

Document ID: n-fee-04  
Published: 2026-06-02  
Source: Dvin Municipality

By decision of the Dvin Municipal Council, the fee for the service listed under reference `market-stall` is revised from 16,000 AMD to 21,000 AMD, effective 2026-06-02. Applications submitted on or after that date are charged at the revised rate. Earlier published schedules are superseded to the extent of this revision.
