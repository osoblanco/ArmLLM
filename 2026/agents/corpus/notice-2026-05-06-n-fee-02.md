# Revised fee schedule — Bulk waste collection booking

Document ID: n-fee-02  
Published: 2026-05-06  
Source: Dvin Municipality

By decision of the Dvin Municipal Council, the fee for the service listed under reference `bulk-waste` is revised from 5,500 AMD to 7,000 AMD, effective 2026-05-06. Applications submitted on or after that date are charged at the revised rate. Earlier published schedules are superseded to the extent of this revision.
