# Revised fee schedule — Commercial signage permit

Document ID: n-fee-03  
Published: 2026-03-19  
Source: Dvin Municipality

By decision of the Dvin Municipal Council, the fee for the service listed under reference `signage-permit` is revised from 20,000 AMD to 26,000 AMD, effective 2026-03-19. Applications submitted on or after that date are charged at the revised rate. Earlier published schedules are superseded to the extent of this revision.
