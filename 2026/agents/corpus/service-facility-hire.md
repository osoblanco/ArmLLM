# School facility hire

Document ID: service-facility-hire  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `facility-hire`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Education Facilities (DEF)  
**Fee:** 12,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 10 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- hire schedule
- public liability insurance

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Garni Citizen Office, 48 Anania Shirakatsi Street (Garni)
- Armavir Service Point, 9 Vardan Mamikonyan Avenue (Armavir)

## Notes

Applications for school facility hire are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for school facility hire is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the school facility hire application has begun.
