# Demolition permit

Document ID: service-demolition-permit  
Published: 2026-01-15  
Source: Dvin Municipality

**Service reference:** `demolition-permit`  
**Responsible department:** Department of Urban Planning and Permits (DUPP)  
**Fee:** 45,000 AMD  
**Processing time:** 30 working days  
**Available online:** no

## Required documents

- structural survey
- waste disposal plan
- land title

## Where to apply

- Dvin Central Service Hall, 12 Trdat Street (Artashat)
- Artashat District Annex, 5 Mashtots Way (Artashat)
- Tigranakert Service Point, 17 Yeghishe Street (Tigranakert)
- Dvin Permits House, 1 Katholikos Square (Garni)

## Notes

Applications for demolition permit are processed in order of receipt. Incomplete submissions are returned without charge. The fee for demolition permit is payable at the time of submission and is non-refundable once processing of the demolition permit application has begun.
