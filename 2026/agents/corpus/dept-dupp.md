# Department of Urban Planning and Permits

Document ID: dept-dupp  
Published: 2026-01-08  
Source: Dvin Municipality

The Department of Urban Planning and Permits (DUPP) is responsible for the services listed below.

**Head of department:** Vahagn Petrosyan  
**Location:** Central Service Hall, floor 4  
**Email:** dupp@dvin.gov.am  
**Opening hours:** Monday to Friday 08:30-16:30; Saturday closed

## Services administered by DUPP

| Service | Reference | Fee | Processing time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction permit | `build-permit` | 85,000 AMD | 45 working days |
| Zoning certificate | `zoning-cert` | 15,000 AMD | 14 working days |
| Facade alteration permit | `facade-permit` | 25,000 AMD | 21 working days |
| Demolition permit | `demolition-permit` | 45,000 AMD | 30 working days |
| Street address assignment | `addr-assign` | 3,000 AMD | 7 working days |
